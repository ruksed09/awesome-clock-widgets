## Versity 0.6.2

**Adding widgets from the app**
- When you add a widget from the studio, your home screen's add prompt now shows the widget as you designed it
- Once you're back in Versity, the Widgets page says whether the widget went on. Home screens such as Moto's put new widgets in the first free space, which can be on another page
- If your home screen doesn't add the widget, the Widgets page explains how to add it yourself, and the look you designed is applied to it automatically
- If your home screen opens Versity's widget setup while placing a widget you just designed, your design is applied straight away. The studio no longer reopens, and the widget is no longer dropped when the studio is closed

**Editing widgets**
- A widget you remove from your home screen now leaves Versity's widget list straight away, even when Versity was open in the background
- Widgets your home screen reserved but never showed are no longer listed
- If a widget is removed while you're editing it, the studio switches to adding it again with your look

**Moving widgets on Versity's home screen**
- Dragging a widget that sits close to the clock now moves the widget, not the clock
- Widgets follow your finger all the way to the top and bottom edges, and come back as soon as you drag the other way
