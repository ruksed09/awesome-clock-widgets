## Versity 0.2.0

**Themes and wallpapers**
- 48 themes in seven groups: Night sky, Nature, Minimal, Bold, Elegant, Glass and clay, Big clock
- 14 bundled wallpapers: Leviathan (crystal whales), cosmic scenes (twin worlds, nebula veins, ringed giant, supernova), glass, silk, dunes and clay pebbles
- Apply any theme or wallpaper to the home screen, the lock screen or both, and optionally to the phone's own wallpapers

**Clocks**
- Big clock faces: Giant, Outline, Neon, 3D, LED, Flip, Stage and Glass, plus the Ornament styles (celestial line, moon divider and more)
- Move and resize the clock anywhere on the home or lock screen, with snapping guides

**App**
- Opens to a hub with Themes, Wallpapers, Widgets and More, in a glass and clay design with light and dark modes
- The themed lock screen follows the phone's real lock: unlocking still uses your PIN, fingerprint or face
- The app drawer and app icons stay stock unless you choose to theme them
- Clock widgets for other home screens

**Install**
Download `versity-0.2.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time.
