## Versity 0.5.0

**Tablets**
- A sidebar replaces the hub on tablets: Themes, Wallpapers, Widgets and Settings are always one tap away
- Settings opens side by side with its pages on wide screens
- Theme and wallpaper sheets open as centred dialogs; galleries show bigger previews
- A tablet held upright keeps one centred home-screen column

**Your own wallpapers, without Versity as your home app**
- Add any photo in Wallpapers and set it as your phone's home or lock-screen wallpaper, or both
- Your photos stay in Versity so you can apply them again; remove any you no longer want
- Wallpapers and photos Versity set on your phone are marked Current while they are still there
- Without Versity as your home app, sheets simply set your phone's wallpaper, with no Versity-only options

**Nothing chosen for you**
- No theme is marked as applied until you apply one; the hub shows "No theme yet" on a fresh install

**Analogue widget**
- Seven dials: Index, Minimal, Dots, Bars, Quarters, Numbers and Roman
- Numerals in any of the ten typefaces, three hand styles, and a round or rounded-square face
- Dial colours work on every Android version; hands follow your colours on Android 12 and newer

**System look where it belongs**
- The plain app drawer follows your phone's light or dark mode and, on Android 12 and newer, its wallpaper colours
- Versity always uses its own fonts, never the phone's font style; text grows with your font size up to 115%

**Updates from GitHub**
- Versity checks for a new version once a day and offers Download and Install; turn it off or check now in Settings
- Downloads are checked against GitHub's checksum and Versity's signing key before installing

**Install**
Download `versity-0.5.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It installs over 0.4.0 and keeps your settings, widgets and photos. From now on, updates can come straight from Settings > Updates.
