## Versity 0.3.0

**Works on every screen size**
- Tablets and wide screens get landscape renderings of every generated wallpaper, so nothing is stretched or awkwardly cropped
- Big clocks fit the height as well as the width, so they never run off the screen in landscape or split screen
- Clock widgets size their text to the cell they are given, from a small 2x1 to a large tablet widget, and use a short date when narrow
- Customise keeps a comfortable reading width on tablets; the hub shows four cards across on tablets, two on phones and one on very narrow cover screens
- Fixed the dock appearing at the top of the screen on tablets and phones in landscape

**12 new wallpapers, 26 in all**
- Minimal: Dawn mesh, Ink mesh, Calm sea, Halo (true black for OLED screens)
- Line art: Contour, Ridgeline, Bauhaus
- Landscapes: Papercut, Misty peaks
- Glass and material: Glass orbs, Liquid chrome, Iridescent

**12 new themes, 60 in all**
Topo, Ridgeline, Papercut, Chrome, Holo, Halo, Dawn, Shoreline, Alpenglow, Composition, Orbs and Ink

**Install**
Download `versity-0.3.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It installs over 0.2.0 and keeps your settings.
