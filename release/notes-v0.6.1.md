## Versity 0.6.1

**Sharper app launcher**
- Every shape is now drawn as an exact outline instead of a softened image, so edges are crisp at any size
- The Flower's joins are smooth single curves, the Cross has rounded inside corners and the Tile has eased squircle corners
- Space is kept around each shape, so shadows and edges are never cut off at the widget's border
- App icons are taken at their highest resolution, and a fine hairline keeps solid shapes clean against dark wallpapers

**New: Spinner**
- Eight apps on a wheel with a ticked rim, a pointer at twelve and a hub
- On Versity's home screen and in the widget studio, turn it with your finger: flick it and it glides to rest on an app, with a light tick as each one passes
- The app under the pointer is shown larger; tap any app to open it
- On other home screen apps, which can't follow a finger, tap the hub to turn the wheel one step
- Works with a solid, glass or no background, round icons and zero to three rings, and remembers where you left it

**Updates**
- New versions now show up within the hour of being published, instead of up to a day later
- An update that has been found stays on screen every time you open Versity until you install it

**Studio**
- Layout choices fill the card in an even grid
- Widget cards show their full names
- Ring count sits in line with the other size options
