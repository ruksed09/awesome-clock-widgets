## Versity 0.4.0

A cleaner, calmer Versity with widgets you design yourself.

**Widget studio**
- Four widget families: Digital clock, Clock and date, Analogue clock, and Date and calendar
- Thirteen layouts across them, from a single line of time to a month at a glance
- Ten typefaces, text and accent colours (or any custom hue), two-tone minutes
- Background: none, glass or a solid colour, with opacity, corner style and a hairline edge
- Alignment, 12 or 24-hour time, four date formats, three ornaments, an optional seconds hand
- A live preview on your own wallpaper, at four widget sizes, on light and dark backdrops
- Re-edit any placed widget: from Widgets in Versity, or by long-pressing it on your home screen
- With Versity as your home app, widgets go straight onto Versity's home screen, where you can move, resize, edit or remove them

**A new look**
- Clean, neutral design throughout: flat surfaces, hairline borders, one accent colour, light and dark
- A new hub, and a Settings page grouped into Home screen, Lock screen, Apps and Appearance
- Clock faces are chosen from live previews; colours and font have their own page with home and lock previews
- One editor arranges the clock and widgets together, with snapping guides

**Home screen**
- A slim dock for your favourite apps; swipe up anywhere for all apps
- Removed the old battery bar, date ring, small dial, notifications chip and drawn backdrops
- Removed the Classic layout and the plain Stacked and Single-line clocks; the analogue face is redrawn
- 41 themes, each built around a wallpaper or a clean gradient

**Install**
Download `versity-0.4.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It installs over 0.3.0 and keeps your settings and the widgets you have placed.
