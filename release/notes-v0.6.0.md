## Versity 0.6.0

**A new widget collection** and more analogue clocks. The collection is designed after a dark, minimal reference: deep black and green, layered rings, soft shadows.

**Countdown**
- Your event's name, the date, a days pill and a live hours, minutes and seconds timer
- Counts down on its own without waking your phone; the day count rolls over on time
- Card or Big days layout; emerald or any accent colour; solid, glass or no panel, corners and edge

**App launcher**
- Five apps on a radial shape: top, left, centre, right and bottom
- Five shapes: Flower (one organic silhouette), Rings, Orbit (a round plate with an orbit line), Cross and Tile
- With or without a background: solid, frosted glass, or none, so the apps float on your wallpaper
- Round icons for a matching set, a larger centre app, and zero to three rings per app
- Choose every app, icon size, spacing, ring thickness, colours, opacity and shadow
- Tapping a circle opens its app

**App shortcut**
- One app in layered rings, or with a soft halo; on a solid, glass or no disc; tap to open

**Badge**
- A scalloped, petal or round badge with an inner ring and your own logo or picture, or an app's icon
- Optional tap to open an app

All four are in Widgets under Collection and in your home screen's widget list, with a live preview while you design them. With Versity as your home app they go straight onto its home screen.

**Analogue clock**
- Five new dials: Railway, Sector, Diver, Segments and Dual (hours inside, minutes outside)
- Three new hand styles: Needle, Dauphine and Skeleton
- Twelve dials and six hand styles in all, on round or rounded-square faces
